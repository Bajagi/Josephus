The Josephus Circle Simulation

by James Lee

A short history

The puzzle is named after Flavius Josephus, a first-century Jewish historian and military commander. In his account of the siege of Yodfat (67 CE), he describes being trapped in a cave with a band of fellow soldiers, who decided to die rather than be captured. They agreed to draw lots and eliminate one another in turn. Josephus and one other man were the last two left, and they chose to surrender instead.

Single N vs. Range of N

Single N simulates one circle of n people, where every d-th person is eliminated. You can step through each elimination and see who survives.

Range of N runs the same game for every group size from 2 up to your chosen maximum and lists the winning position for each. Click a row to see that group's full elimination order.

How to play

  1. Gather n people in a circle and number them 0 to n-1.
  2. Pick a step size d. Person 0 says “1”, the next says “2”, and so on.
  3. Whoever says d steps out. Counting restarts at the next person still standing.
  4. Keep going until one person is left. That person wins.
Person 0 says “1”, so with d = 2, person 1 is the first one out.
